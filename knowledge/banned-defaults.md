# Defaults to avoid

Without concrete constraints a model drifts to the same template. Name what to
avoid in your brief; break a rule only on purpose, when the subject asks for it.

## The template look

- Dark background + centred big headline + gradient glow + everything fading in.
- All elements entering at once with the same fade and the same duration.
- Equal-weight cards in a row, each with an icon, a title and a sentence.
- Corner labels, frame borders, "REC" dots and HUD brackets with no meaning.
- Glows and gradients on UI chrome; bevelled glass panels everywhere.
- A traveling line or orbiting ring used as the only connection between scenes.
- Particle explosions as punctuation; lens flares as decoration.
- Bouncy, overshooting easing on everything.
- Linear sliding; dead time where nothing moves.
- Zoom-blurred text; text scaled until it softens.
- Stock slogans: "The future is here", "Unleash", "Revolutionize", "Join us".
- Fake UI data, fake logos, fake QR codes.

## The cheap transition kit

Default crossfades and slide wipes as the only transitions; the same transition
repeated at every boundary; a fade to black between every section.

## Structure

- Opening on a logo fade before anything happens.
- A list of facts with no turn, no escalation and no release.
- Ending on a busy frame with the call to action too small or too brief to read.

## What to do instead

Pick a specific visual world from the subject, a strong typographic system, a
camera, real media used boldly, transitions that are made of the content, a
rhythm with contrast, and a final destination that holds long enough to act on.
