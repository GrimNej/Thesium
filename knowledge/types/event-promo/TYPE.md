# Event Promo — Video Type

> The promo for a conference, summit, festival, hackathon, meetup or launch event: make it feel
> unmissable and get people to register. Grammar benchmarks (copy nothing): Apple WWDC openers,
> SXSW and Web Summit promos, Config and Vercel Ship trailers, music-festival lineup reveals.

## 1. The job, and what it is not

- **Job:** registrations and ticket sales. Success: clicks to tickets and shares.
- **Audience:** people who could attend; they need the why, the who, the when and the where.
- **Not** an agenda read aloud; energy and essentials, then the ticket.

## 2. Beat structure

| Length | Beats (seconds) |
|---|---|
| 15 s | Name + date 0–3 · three highlights (speakers, tracks, scale) 3–11 · venue + tickets 11–15 |
| 30 s | Hook (the theme or a striking image of the city/venue) 0–4 · name, dates, city 4–8 · speakers 8–15 · tracks or experiences 15–22 · scale numbers (sourced) 22–25 · venue + tickets CTA 25–30 |
| 60 s | Theme 0–8 · the event identity 8–14 · speakers 14–26 · programme 26–40 · community and past edition 40–50 · venue, dates, tickets, QR 50–60 |

## 3. Must-haves

- Event name, dates, city and venue exactly as sourced, early (within 8 s) and again at the end.
- Speakers with real photos, names and affiliations as provided.
- Tracks or programme highlights by their real names.
- Ticket URL or QR held ≥ 3 s, with the registration deadline or price if sourced.

## 4. Motion and visual language

- **Event identity in motion**: the event's brand colours, shapes and type animate as a system;
  the date and city become graphic heroes (huge numerals, map pins, skylines).
- **Speaker reveals**: portraits arriving in a rhythmic grid, cut-outs with brand-colour backgrounds,
  names and roles sliding in on the beat; groups always centred.
- **Place**: the venue or city shown with real photos treated with parallax and depth, or built in
  code (skyline, map flight) when no photo exists.
- **Energy curve**: build to the lineup, peak on the scale numbers, land on the ticket card.
- Signature options: a map flight from the world to the venue; the date built from the stage lights;
  the theme word transforming through each track.

## 5. Type and copy

Bold event type, giant dates, speaker names readable on a phone, tracks as short labels, a strong
ticket CTA.

## 6. Sound

Driving, celebratory track at 120–130 BPM (electronic or percussive), hits on each speaker and number,
a crowd-swell or riser into the ticket card.

## 7. Formats and delivery

16:9 for the site and screens at the event; 9:16 and 1:1 for social. In vertical, stack the lineup.

## 8. What to take from the sources

Name, theme, dates, city, venue, speakers (photos, names, roles), tracks, schedule highlights,
attendance and past-edition numbers, sponsors, ticket URL, prices and deadlines, brand assets.

## 9. Pitfalls

- Dates or venue missing, wrong or shown too small.
- Unreadable speaker cards; too many speakers per frame.
- Showing the wrong building for the venue.

## 10. Variation space

- Openings: the city at night; the theme word; a countdown to the date.
- Structures: a lineup reveal; a day at the event; past edition → this edition.
- Endings: venue + date + QR; the logo drops into the city; a crowd of avatars forming the ticket button.

## 11. Pairs well with

Pictogram Motion, Swiss Motion Graphics, Silkscreen Travel Poster, 60s Spy Title Sequence, Art Deco
(galas), Dark Tech Keynote (tech events).
