# Performance Social Ad — Video Type

> A paid ad for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Meta feeds, built to stop the scroll and earn the
> click: hook in the first second, product early, one message, a hard call to action, readable
> with the sound off. Grammar benchmarks (copy nothing): top-performing DTC and app ads, TikTok
> Top Ads, Meta Creative Center best practices.

## 1. The job, and what it is not

- **Job:** one measurable action (install, sign-up, purchase). Success is the hook rate (viewers
  who pass 3 s), click-through and cost per result.
- **Audience:** strangers scrolling fast, mostly with the sound off.
- **Not** a brand film and not a slow build: if the first second is not interesting, the ad is
  dead.

## 2. Beat structure

| Length | Beats (seconds) |
|---|---|
| 6 s | Hook + product 0–2 · benefit 2–4 · CTA 4–6 |
| 15 s | Hook 0–2 · problem or desire 2–4 · product in action 4–10 · proof (only if sourced: rating, number, quote) 10–12 · offer + CTA 12–15 |
| 30 s | Hook 0–3 · context 3–7 · demo or transformation 7–20 · social proof 20–25 · offer + CTA 25–30 |

The **product is on screen by 2 s**. The CTA holds for the final 2–3 s with a clear verb.

## 3. Must-haves

- A pattern-breaking first frame: motion already happening, a bold claim in huge type, a
  before/after, a surprising object or an extreme close-up.
- Burned-in text for every message (sound off), large and high contrast.
- One message and one offer, exactly as sourced (price, discount, free trial).
- A CTA that names the action: "Try it free", "Shop now", "Get the app".

## 4. Motion and visual language

- **Something changes every 0.5–1.5 s**: cuts, punch-ins, type swaps, reveals on the beat.
- **Kinetic captions**: words pop one by one, highlight bars sweep under key words, icons spring in.
- **Transformation on screen**: before → after wipes, sliders, counters, bars filling.
- **Premium but native**: polished, yet at home in a feed; never a TV spot shrunk down.
- Signature options: a punch-in on the product with a flash; a split screen "the old way vs this";
  a thumb-stopping macro of the product with the claim stamped across it.

## 5. Type and copy

Chunky sans, 2–6 words per screen, at most two lines, the key word in the accent colour. Hook
formulas: a question, a bold claim, a number, a problem statement, "POV:".

## 6. Sound

High-energy music at 120–140 BPM, punchy hits on cuts, swooshes, a pop per caption word, and the
ad must still work fully muted.

## 7. Formats and delivery

9:16 master (1080×1920): keep text and product in the centre safe zone, away from the top 14 % and
bottom 20 % where platform UI sits. 1:1 and 4:5 cuts optional.

## 8. What to take from the sources

Product, offer, price, real reviews or ratings (only if published), the main benefit, landing URL,
product photos or app screens, brand colours.

## 9. Pitfalls

- A logo intro or a slow fade-in at the start.
- The product hidden until the end.
- Text in the platform UI zones or too small to read on a phone.
- Invented testimonials, ratings or scarcity ("only 3 left").

## 10. Variation space

- Hooks: "Stop doing X"; a satisfying action loop; the result first; a question in huge type; a
  counterintuitive number.
- Structures: problem → solution; three reasons; before/after; a mini unboxing; a listicle.
- Endings: an offer card; the product and a CTA button pressing itself; a loop back to the hook so
  it rewatches seamlessly.

## 11. Pairs well with

Microgame Frenzy, Game Show Flat, Swiss Motion Graphics, Glass Product Render, Halftone Dossier
(bold editorial ads).
