# Video types

A **video type** is what a film is for and how it is built: its job, its beat structure for each
length, its must-haves, motion and sound conventions, and its pitfalls. A **style**
([`../styles/`](../styles/README.md)) is how it looks. A film can have both: a Product Launch Film in
Swiss Motion Graphics, an Event Promo in Silkscreen Travel Poster.

When a film has a type, the brief points the director to its `TYPE.md`, the planning pass receives
its beats, and the independent review checks the film delivers them.
