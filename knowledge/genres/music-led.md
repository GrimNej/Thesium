# Music-led pieces (music videos, sizzle reels, showreels)

- The track is the script: map sections (intro, verse, build, drop, break, outro) first.
- Cut on downbeats; change the visual system at section changes; accent hits with flashes,
  punch-ins and shakes.
- Lyrics or words as typography: karaoke reveals, word slams on hits, letters that
  become shapes.
- Visual vocabulary for drops: punch zoom, chromatic split, whip with blur, strobe of
  alternating compositions, glitch slices, light leak flashes. Use each sparingly so it lands.
- Keep a home base the film returns to between excursions.
