# Event promo

**Job:** make someone feel they cannot miss it, then tell them exactly when, where
and how to get in. Energy first, information second, the action last and longest.

## Coverage to check in the sources

Name and edition · dates (and timezone if relevant) · city and venue · scale
(speakers, attendees, sessions) · themes or tracks · headline speakers · format
(keynotes, workshops, hackathons, demos, side events) · organisers, sponsors and
partners · past editions and their photos · registration URL and ticket notes
(early bird, student) · call for proposals · hero video and photography.
Mark each supported, missing or irrelevant. Never invent a speaker, date or price.

## Shape that works (adapt, do not copy)

| Phase | Share | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0–8% | A specific, arresting image or word from the event's world, on the first downbeat |
| Identity | 8–20% | Name, edition, city, dates land as a designed lockup |
| Proof of energy | 20–55% | Real photos/video of past editions, scale numbers, tracks as a kinetic system |
| Substance | 55–75% | Tracks, workshops, hackathons, speakers: grouped, rhythmic, readable |
| Community | 75–85% | Sponsors/partners as a choreographed wall, organisers, side events |
| Call to action | 85–100% | Dates + venue + URL (and QR if provided), held ≥ 4 s, music resolving |

## Techniques that suit events

- **Kinetic dates and numbers:** counters that land on the beat (150+, 2,000+), split-flap or
  odometer digits, giant numerals with tiny labels.
- **Venue as a world:** a city skyline drawn in lines, a map that zooms to the venue, a
  landmark extruded in 3D, real venue photography in parallax layers.
- **Photo energy:** fast beat-cut montages of past-edition photos inside a strong grid,
  masks shaped like the event's mark, Ken Burns with motion blur, duotone in brand colours.
- **Tracks as a system:** each track gets a glyph, colour and motion verb; they assemble into
  one structure (orbit, grid, network, stack) that becomes the logo or date.
- **Sponsor wall:** logos arrive in rows on the beat, on a light or neutral panel where the
  logos read, held long enough to recognise; never over busy imagery.
- **Signature move:** one transformation the audience remembers (the date assembling from
  track glyphs; the camera flying through the venue's window into the stage; the logo
  stroke becoming the skyline).
- **Bilingual events:** set the second language as a typographic partner (smaller weight,
  aligned to the same grid), not an afterthought subtitle.

## Sound

Driving tempo (118–132 BPM) with a build into a drop at the proof-of-energy section, a
breakdown or near-silence before the final lift, and a button ending on the call to action.
Impacts on numbers, whooshes on cuts, a riser before the date reveal.
