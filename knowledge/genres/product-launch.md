# Product launch

**Job:** show the change the product makes, make it desirable, end on how to get it.

- Lead with the user's problem or the product's single most striking moment, not the logo.
- Real UI from captures or supplied screens; push the camera onto the actual button, value
  or interaction. Rebuild key UI in DOM when you need it to move; keep it faithful.
- One-shape UI morphs, cursor-driven interactions on the beat, springs, motion blur.
- Feature beats: one feature per beat, a verb and a visible consequence ("Search 10,000
  docs" → results resolve), not a card grid.
- Device frames are optional; full-bleed UI with a strong crop often reads better.
- Close: product name, one-line promise, URL or call to action, held ≥ 3 s.
- Sound: clean, tight, rhythmic; UI clicks and pops on interactions; a confident ending hit.
