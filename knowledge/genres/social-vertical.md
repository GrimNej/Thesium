# Vertical social (9:16)

- Design for the phone: the subject in the central 60% of height; keep the top 12% and
  bottom 20% clear for app chrome and captions.
- Hook in the first second; first frame works as a thumbnail.
- Big type (≥ 90 px at 1080 wide), few words, high contrast.
- Faster rhythm, but keep 1.8 s minimum holds for text.
- Loop-friendly endings help retention: make the last frame lead back to the first.
