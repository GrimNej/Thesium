# Explainer

**Job:** make one mechanism click. Accuracy beats spectacle.

- Start with a question or a concrete situation; end with the answer applied.
- One visual protagonist that carries the idea (a token, a packet, a drop of water) across steps.
- Show cause and consequence: every motion changes something the viewer can see.
- Build diagrams progressively; never reveal the whole diagram at once.
- Labels big and few; hold each long enough to read; one new concept per beat.
- Use SVG/Canvas for precise diagrams, Three.js for spatial structure, Manim-style
  transforms (morphing shapes, equations rewriting themselves) where they clarify.
- Facts only from sources; uncertain claims removed and listed in the review log.
- Sound: understated pulse, soft clicks on reveals, space for reading.
