Quality
Five gates between a draft and your screen.
The model judges its own work from pictures and numbers. Thesium makes sure those exist, and stops anything that breaks the rules.
1
Every change

Check

  • The film matches its size, length and engine
  • No clocks, no randomness, no files from the web
  • The same frame comes out every time
  • On-screen text in the brief's language
2
Every preview

Frame check

  • Empty or flat frames
  • Black bands at the edges
  • Pictures that stop short of the frame
3
Every master

Master QA

  • Length, codec, frame rate, size
  • Loudness −14 LUFS, peaks under −1 dB
  • Long black stretches
  • Voice clearly above the music, every word heard
4
After a master

Critic

  • A reviewer that did not make the film
  • Scores it against a rubric
  • Gives timed notes for one polish pass
5
Any time

You

  • Watch every version in the review studio
  • Pin notes on the timeline, each with its frame
  • Tune the colours and words the film exposes
